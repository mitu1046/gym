Monday — Push
Chest · Shoulders · Triceps  |  60 min weights + 30 min cardio
1
Machine Chest Press
3 sets × 12 repsRest 60 secLight–Moderate weight
how to do it
  • Sit on the machine, adjust seat so handles are at chest height
  • Grip handles with palms facing forward, feet flat on floor
  • Push handles forward until arms are almost straight — don't lock out
  • Slowly bring handles back until you feel a stretch in your chest (2 sec)
  • Keep your back flat against the pad throughout — don't arch
alternatives
Dumbbell Chest PressSmith Machine BenchPush-ups
2
Machine Shoulder Press
3 sets × 12 repsRest 60 secLight weight
how to do it
  • Adjust seat so handles are at shoulder height
  • Grip handles, elbows bent at ~90°, core tight
  • Press upward until arms are almost fully extended
  • Lower slowly back to start — don't drop the weight
  • Breathe out when pushing up, breathe in when lowering
alternatives
Dumbbell Shoulder PressArnold PressCable Overhead Press
3
Cable Tricep Pushdown
3 sets × 15 repsRest 45 secLight weight
how to do it
  • Stand at cable machine, attach rope or straight bar at top pulley
  • Grip the attachment, elbows locked close to your sides — they must NOT move
  • Push the bar/rope down until arms are fully straight
  • Slowly let it rise back up until forearms are parallel to floor
  • Squeeze your triceps hard at the bottom of each rep
alternatives
Dumbbell KickbackOverhead Tricep ExtensionBench Dips
4
Dumbbell Lateral Raise
3 sets × 15 repsRest 45 secVery light (5–8 kg)
how to do it
  • Stand holding dumbbells at your sides, slight bend in elbows
  • Raise both arms out to the sides until level with shoulders
  • Tilt your pinkies slightly up at the top (like pouring water)
  • Lower slowly in 2–3 seconds — don't let them drop
  • No swinging — keep your body still throughout
alternatives
Cable Lateral RaiseMachine Lateral RaiseBent-over Lateral Raise

Tuesday — Pull
Back · Biceps · Rear Delts  |  60 min weights + 30 min cardio
1
Lat Pulldown
3 sets × 12 repsRest 60 secModerate weight
how to do it
  • Sit at the machine, lock thighs under the pad, grip bar wider than shoulder width
  • Lean back slightly (~15°), chest up — this is your start position
  • Pull the bar down to your upper chest, lead with your elbows going to your hips
  • Squeeze your back/lats hard at the bottom for 1 sec
  • Slowly let the bar rise back up over 2–3 seconds, feel the stretch
alternatives
Assisted Pull-up MachineCable Pulldown (neutral grip)Single-arm Pulldown
2
Seated Cable Row
3 sets × 12 repsRest 60 secModerate weight
how to do it
  • Sit at cable row machine, feet on platform, slight knee bend
  • Grip the handle (V-grip or straight bar), sit up straight — don't hunch
  • Pull the handle to your belly button, squeezing shoulder blades together
  • Your elbows should go past your torso, close to your body
  • Slowly extend arms back to start — don't round your lower back
alternatives
Machine RowDumbbell Row (one arm)T-bar Row
3
Dumbbell Bicep Curl
3 sets × 15 repsRest 45 secLight weight
how to do it
  • Stand holding dumbbells at your sides, palms facing forward
  • Curl both dumbbells up toward your shoulders — elbows stay fixed at your sides
  • Squeeze your biceps hard at the top
  • Lower slowly (3 sec) — don't just drop them
  • No swinging your back — if you are, the weight is too heavy
alternatives
Cable CurlHammer CurlPreacher Curl Machine
4
Face Pull (cable)
3 sets × 15 repsRest 45 secVery light
how to do it
  • Set cable pulley to face/eye height, attach rope handle
  • Grab rope with both hands, thumbs pointing up, step back 2 steps
  • Pull the rope toward your face — split the rope as you pull (hands go to ears)
  • Elbows should stay high (at shoulder level or above)
  • Pause at full pull, then slowly let it extend back
alternatives
Rear Delt Fly (dumbbell)Band Pull-apartReverse Pec Deck

Wednesday — Legs + Core
Quads · Hamstrings · Glutes · Core  |  60 min weights + 30 min cardio
1
Leg Press Machine
4 sets × 15 repsRest 75 secModerate weight
how to do it
  • Sit in the machine, place feet shoulder-width apart on the platform, toes slightly out
  • Feet placement: mid-to-high on the platform for more glutes/hamstrings
  • Release the safety, lower the platform until knees are at ~90° angle
  • Push through your heels — don't let knees cave inward
  • Don't lock your knees at the top — keep a slight bend
alternatives
Goblet Squat (dumbbell)Smith Machine SquatHack Squat Machine
2
Leg Curl Machine (seated or lying)
3 sets × 15 repsRest 60 secLight–Moderate
how to do it
  • Sit/lie on the machine as the label shows, position pad just above ankles
  • Curl your legs back toward your glutes as far as possible
  • Squeeze your hamstrings hard at the peak for 1 sec
  • Lower back slowly — take 2–3 seconds on the way down
  • Don't lift your hips off the seat to cheat the rep
alternatives
Dumbbell Romanian DeadliftNordic Curl (assisted)Cable Pull-through
3
Leg Extension Machine
3 sets × 15 repsRest 60 secLight–Moderate
how to do it
  • Adjust the backrest so your knees hang at the edge of the seat
  • Pad should rest just above your ankles (not on your shins)
  • Extend legs forward until straight, squeeze quads at the top
  • Lower slowly — don't let the weight crash down
  • Toes can point forward or slightly outward — comfortable for you
alternatives
Lunges (bodyweight)Step-ups (on bench)Wall Sit
4
Plank + Crunches (Core)
Plank: 3×30–45 sec · Crunch: 3×20Rest 45 sec
how to do it — plank
  • Forearms on floor, elbows under shoulders, toes on floor
  • Body must be a straight line from head to heels — don't let hips sag or rise
  • Squeeze your belly button toward your spine, breathe normally
how to do it — crunch
  • Lie on back, knees bent, hands lightly behind head (don't pull neck)
  • Curl shoulders off the floor using your abs — only come up ~30–40°
  • Lower back slowly — don't bounce
alternatives
Dead BugBicycle CrunchLeg Raises

Thursday — Upper Body Compound
Full upper body with heavier compound moves  |  60 min weights + 30 min cardio
1
Smith Machine / Assisted Bench Press
3 sets × 10 repsRest 75 secModerate weight
how to do it
  • Lie on bench under the smith machine bar, grip slightly wider than shoulders
  • Arch your back slightly, feet flat, shoulder blades squeezed together and down
  • Unrack the bar, lower it slowly to your lower chest (2–3 sec)
  • Press back up explosively — don't bounce off your chest
  • Smith machine is safer for beginners — the bar is on a fixed track
alternatives
Dumbbell Bench PressMachine Chest PressIncline Dumbbell Press
2
Dumbbell Row (one arm)
3 sets × 12 reps each sideRest 60 secModerate weight
how to do it
  • Place left knee and left hand on bench for support, right foot on floor
  • Hold dumbbell in right hand, let it hang straight down
  • Pull the dumbbell up toward your hip, elbow going up and back
  • Squeeze your back at the top — hold 1 sec
  • Lower slowly. Repeat on other side
alternatives
Machine RowCable RowMeadows Row
3
Dumbbell Shoulder Press (seated)
3 sets × 12 repsRest 60 secLight–Moderate
how to do it
  • Sit on an incline bench (set upright), hold dumbbells at shoulder height
  • Palms facing forward, elbows at ~90° to start
  • Press both dumbbells upward until arms are nearly straight
  • Lower them slowly back to shoulder height — don't go below
  • Keep your core braced — don't lean back excessively
alternatives
Machine Shoulder PressCable Shoulder PressBarbell OHP (later)
4
Assisted Tricep Dips / Bench Dips
3 sets × 12 repsRest 60 secBodyweight or assisted
how to do it — bench dip (easier)
  • Sit on edge of bench, hands gripping edge beside your hips, feet forward
  • Slide your butt off the bench, lower your body by bending elbows to ~90°
  • Push back up — don't flare elbows too wide
  • Keep your back close to the bench throughout
  • To make it harder: put feet on another bench
alternatives
Cable PushdownOverhead DB ExtensionAssisted Dip Machine

Friday — Full Body + Core Burn
Compound full-body circuit + core finisher  |  60 min weights + 30 min cardio
1
Goblet Squat (dumbbell)
4 sets × 15 repsRest 60 secLight–Moderate
how to do it
  • Hold one dumbbell vertically at your chest with both hands (like a goblet)
  • Feet shoulder-width, toes pointing out 30–45°
  • Squat down — chest up, knees pushed outward over toes
  • Go as deep as comfortable (aim for thighs parallel to floor)
  • Drive through heels to stand back up, squeeze glutes at top
alternatives
Leg PressSmith Machine SquatBodyweight Squat
2
Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift (RDL)
3 sets × 12 repsRest 75 secLight–Moderate
how to do it
  • Stand holding dumbbells in front of you, feet hip-width apart
  • Slight bend in knees — keep it throughout, this is NOT a squat
  • Hinge at hips — push your butt backward, let dumbbells slide down your legs
  • Lower until you feel a stretch in your hamstrings (usually mid-shin level)
  • Drive your hips forward to stand back up — squeeze glutes at the top
alternatives
Leg Curl MachineCable Pull-throughKettlebell Swing
3
Cable Crossover / Chest Fly
3 sets × 15 repsRest 45 secVery light
how to do it
  • Set cables to high position, stand in the middle of the machine
  • Hold both handles, step forward, arms spread wide — slight bend in elbows
  • Bring hands together in front of your chest in a hugging arc motion
  • Hold for 1 sec squeezing your chest, then slowly open arms back out
  • Keep elbows slightly bent throughout — never fully straight
alternatives
Dumbbell Chest FlyPec Deck MachineLow Cable Fly
